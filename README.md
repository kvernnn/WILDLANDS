# WILDLANDS

## Game Length and Player Count

WILDLANDS supports **3–6 players**. A 3-player game should last approximately 20 minutes; games with more players will usually take 25–35 minutes.

| Players | Central Deck | Round Limit | Estimated Time |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 3 | 30 cards | 5 rounds | About 20 minutes |
| 4 | 40 cards | 4 rounds | About 20–25 minutes |
| 5 | 50 cards | 3 rounds | About 25–30 minutes |
| 6 | 60 cards | 3 rounds | About 30–35 minutes |

A round ends after every player has taken one turn. See [End of the Game](#end-of-the-game) for the other end conditions.

## Game Setup

### Board Size

Build a connected, roughly circular board using the number of terrain tiles shown below. The larger boards give additional players enough room to spread out without making interaction or exploration too rare.

| Players | Total Terrain Tiles | Initially Face-Up | Initially Face-Down |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 3 | 20 | 7 | 13 |
| 4 | 24 | 7 | 17 |
| 5 | 28 | 7 | 21 |
| 6 | 32 | 7 | 25 |

Use the six standard terrain types as evenly as possible across the full board. Always include both neutral tiles.

| Players | Standard-Terrain Distribution | Neutral Tiles |
| ---: | --- | ---: |
| 3 | 3 of each standard terrain | 2 |
| 4 | 4 each of four randomly selected terrain types; 3 each of the other two | 2 |
| 5 | 5 each of two randomly selected terrain types; 4 each of the other four | 2 |
| 6 | 5 of each standard terrain | 2 |

For 4- and 5-player games, determine the terrain types receiving additional copies randomly before players see the board.

### Revealed Central Core

The recommended board uses **hexagonal terrain tiles**, allowing six equally connected starting terrains around one central tile. Create a symmetrical seven-tile starting area:

1. Place the **Great Watering Hole** face-up in the centre.
2. Take one Highlands, Plains, Wetlands, Forest, River, and Tundra tile. Shuffle them and place all six face-up around the Great Watering Hole.
3. Shuffle the **Ancient Grove** into the remaining terrain tiles.
4. Place every remaining tile face-down in connected outer layers around the revealed core. Avoid gaps and ensure every outer tile connects to the rest of the board.

The six standard terrains are shuffled so no animal always begins beside the same secondary terrain. Seven revealed tiles are recommended instead of only six because a central neutral hub makes every starting position equally connected. Revealing more than the central seven weakens Explore and the Pathfinder title.

### Player Setup and Starting Positions

1. Each player chooses a different animal and takes its Animal Player Board, animal pieces, structures, Settlement markers, and VP slider.
2. Set every VP slider to 0. Give each player **2 Food and 1 Material**.
3. Place starting animal pieces according to the table below.
4. Shuffle the completed central deck and deal **5 cards** to each player.
5. Randomly choose the first player. Play proceeds clockwise.

| Animal | Starting Pieces | Starting Tile |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Eagle | 1 | Revealed Highlands tile |
| Bear | 1 | Revealed Forest tile |
| Crocodile | 1 | Revealed River tile |
| Wolf | 2 | Revealed Tundra tile |
| Frog | 2 | Revealed Wetlands tile |
| Hare | 2 | Revealed Plains tile |
| Rat | 4 | Great Watering Hole |

For every non-Rat animal, the starting number is one below its pack size. This keeps each animal one growth step away from building its first structure. The Rat begins one piece below Infestation. Do not award Pathfinder tokens for tiles revealed during setup.

## Terrain Tiles

The standard terrain types are:

- Highlands
- Plains
- Wetlands
- Forest
- River
- Tundra

Two additional neutral terrain tiles are also used:

- Great Watering Hole
- Ancient Grove

Terrain tiles produce **Food** or **Materials** when foraged, depending on the terrain's relationship to the foraging animal. Certain animal abilities modify how resources are collected. See [Animals](#animals) and [Forage](#1-forage).

### Neutral Tiles

The Great Watering Hole and Ancient Grove are placed during setup and remain fixed for the entire game.

- Every animal treats these as neutral terrain.
- When foraging on a neutral tile, collect **2 resources** in any combination: **2 Food**, **2 Materials**, or **1 Food and 1 Material**.
- Structures cannot be built on neutral tiles.
- Neutral tiles are shared gathering and negotiation spaces. No player can claim an exclusive terrain advantage there.

## Animals

| Animal | Primary Terrain | Secondary Terrain | Pack Size | Total Tokens |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Eagle | Highlands | Plains | 2 | 4 |
| Bear | Forest | Highlands | 2 | 6 |
| Crocodile | River | Wetlands | 2 | 6 |
| Wolf | Tundra | Forest | 3 | 9 |
| Frog | Wetlands | River | 3 | 9 |
| Hare | Plains | Tundra | 3 | 9 |
| Rat | See **Infestation** | See **Infestation** | 5 | 12 |

Each animal has the following attributes:

- **Home terrain:** The animal's primary and secondary terrains, where it forages more efficiently and may build structures.
- **Pack size:** The minimum number of that animal's tokens required on a tile to build a structure there.
- **Spawn rate:** The maximum number of animal tokens that may be placed during one **Reproduce** action.
- **Passive ability:** An ability that is always active and requires no action.
- **Active abilities:** Abilities that can be used only by playing the corresponding **Species Ability Card**.

### Animal Player Boards

Each player receives an **Animal Player Board** for their chosen animal. It acts as a rules reference and a score tracker. Each board should show:

- The animal's primary and secondary terrain.
- Pack size, spawn rate, and total number of animal pieces.
- The animal's passive ability and two active abilities.
- A summary of the six standard actions, including their costs and requirements.
- A **0–20 VP track** with a sliding marker. Move the marker whenever VP is gained. Final-scoring bonuses may take a player beyond 20; record any excess beside the board if necessary.

| Action | Cost | Main Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forage | No resource cost | Collect from every tile occupied by your animals; maximum 6 base resources. |
| Move | 1 Food per animal moved | Move each selected animal 1 tile unless modified by an effect. |
| Reproduce | Variable Food cost | Place up to your spawn rate on tiles you occupy. |
| Play a Card | The card and any printed cost | Resolve a card, or place a Secret Card face-down. |
| Build | 1 Food and 2 Materials | Home terrain, pack size met, and fewer than 3 structures in play. |
| Explore | No resource cost | Move one animal onto an adjacent face-down tile and reveal it. |

### Animal Abilities and Interactions

#### Eagle

- **Home terrain:** Highlands (Primary) / Plains (Secondary)
- **Pack size:** 2
- **Spawn rate:** 1
- **Passive — Apex Roamer:** Each Eagle may move **1 additional tile** whenever it moves.
- **Active 1 — Distant Travels:** Requires a **Distant Travels** card. This ability counts as your **Move** action, so you cannot take another **Move** action this turn. You may move each of your Eagles up to **3 tiles**.
- **Active 2 — Kleptoparasitism:** Requires a **Kleptoparasitism** card. This reactive card cannot be played during your own turn. When another animal uses the **Forage** action, you may play it to steal **1 resource** that animal just collected.

#### Bear

- **Home terrain:** Forest (Primary) / Highlands (Secondary)
- **Pack size:** 2
- **Spawn rate:** 1
- **Passive — Omnivore:** When foraging on a Plains tile, the Bear may collect **1 Material** instead of **1 Food**.
- **Active 1 — Salmon Run:** Requires a **Salmon Run** card and must be used as the first action of your turn. Move one Bear token to any River tile, regardless of distance, then immediately collect **1 Food**. Salmon Run counts as your **Move** action, so you cannot take another Move action this turn.
- **Active 2 — Mark Territory:** Requires a **Mark Territory** card. Choose one tile occupied by at least one of your Bears. Until the start of your next turn, that tile is marked. Other animals cannot end a **Move** on or **Forage** from it. Animals already there may still move out.

#### Crocodile

- **Home terrain:** River (Primary) / Wetlands (Secondary)
- **Pack size:** 2
- **Spawn rate:** 1
- **Passive — Lurking Presence:** When another player forages on one or more tiles occupied by your Crocodiles, that player must give you **1 Food** before collecting resources from those tiles. If they cannot pay, they cannot forage from those tiles. This triggers at most once per opponent's **Forage** action.
- **Active 1 — Death Roll:** Requires a **Death Roll** card. Choose one tile occupied by at least one of your Crocodiles. Until the start of your next turn, another animal must give you **2 Food** before it can enter or pass through that tile. If it cannot or will not pay, it cannot enter that tile.
- **Active 2 — High-Speed Land Galloping:** Requires a **High-Speed Land Galloping** card. This ability counts as your **Move** action, so you cannot take another **Move** action this turn. You may move each of your Crocodiles up to **2 tiles**.

#### Wolf

- **Home terrain:** Tundra (Primary) / Forest (Secondary)
- **Pack size:** 3
- **Spawn rate:** 2
- **Passive — Pack Synergy:** Whenever you forage, gain **1 additional Food** for each adjacent tile occupied by at least one of your Wolf tokens, to a maximum of **3 additional Food**.
- **Active 1 — Pack Hunt:** Requires a **Pack Hunt** card. If at least 3 of your Wolf tokens occupy the same tile, collect **2 Food and 1 Material**. You may still take a **Forage** action this turn.
- **Active 2 — Kill Zone:** Requires a **Kill Zone** card. If a tile is adjacent to at least 3, 4, or 5 tiles occupied by your Wolf tokens, collect 1, 2, or 3 resources respectively from each other player with an animal on the surrounded tile.

#### Frog

- **Home terrain:** Wetlands (Primary) / River (Secondary)
- **Pack size:** 3
- **Spawn rate:** 2
- **Passive — Leg Day:** When taking the **Move** action, you may choose to move only one Frog token. That Frog may move **1 additional tile** for each other Frog token you have in play, to a maximum of **2 additional tiles**.
- **Active 1 — Sticky Situation:** Requires a **Sticky Situation** card. Choose one tile occupied by at least one of your Frogs. Until the start of your next turn, all other animals sharing that tile with your Frogs are immobilised and cannot move.
- **Active 2 — Tongue Tug:** Requires a **Tongue Tug** card. Choose another animal within **2 tiles** of one of your Frogs. Pull that animal **1 tile** closer to the Frog. It is then immobilised and cannot move until the start of your next turn.

#### Hare

- **Home terrain:** Plains (Primary) / Tundra (Secondary)
- **Pack size:** 3
- **Spawn rate:** 2
- **Passive — Snowshoe Sprint:** When one of your Hares enters a Plains or Tundra tile during a **Move** action, it may immediately move **1 additional tile** for free. Each Hare can receive this bonus only once during that Move action.
- **Active 1 — Zig-Zag:** Requires a **Zig-Zag** card. This reactive card cannot be played during your own turn. When an opponent targets one of your Hares with an animal ability, play this card to cancel that ability's effects and move the targeted Hare **1 tile** in any direction.
- **Active 2 — Burrow:** Requires a **Burrow** card. If a Hare is on a Plains or Tundra tile, remove it from the board and place it on any other empty Plains or Tundra tile.

#### Rat

- **Home terrain:** None
- **Pack size:** 5
- **Spawn rate:** 2
- **Passive — Infestation:** The Rat has no fixed home terrain. A tile containing at least **5 of your Rat tokens** is treated as your home terrain for all purposes. When the number of your Rats on a tile reaches **5**, place one of your structures there for free, provided you have fewer than 3 structures on the board. If the number drops below 5, immediately remove the structure and return it to your supply. A Rat structure awards **1 VP** only if you have not previously scored a structure on that tile. Rats cannot construct structures using the **Build** action. When you **Forage** on an infested tile, choose Food or Materials and collect **1 of the chosen resource per Rat** there, subject to the normal Forage cap.
- **Active 1 — Scurry:** Requires a **Scurry** card. This reactive card cannot be played during your own turn. When an opponent moves an animal token onto a tile containing your Rats, move up to **2 Rat tokens** from that tile to one or more adjacent tiles.
- **Active 2 — Nibble:** Requires a **Nibble** card. If at least 3 of your Rats occupy a tile containing another player's structure, attempt to destroy that structure. If it is unfortified, return it to its owner and gain **2 Food**. If it is fortified, remove its Fortification token instead and do not gain Food.

**Rat structure clarification:** If another player destroys a Rat structure while at least 5 Rats remain on its tile, the structure is rebuilt only after the number of Rats on that tile changes and remains at or above 5. For example: 5 Rats → structure placed → structure destroyed → a sixth Rat moves onto the tile → structure rebuilt. If the count falls below 5, the structure is rebuilt when it later returns to 5. The Rat player's Settlement marker remains on the tile, so a rebuilt Rat structure there does not award VP again.

## Instinct Objectives

Each animal has a public **Instinct Objective** that reinforces its distinct play style. A player immediately gains **3 VP** the first time they complete their objective. Each objective can be scored only once per game, and these points count toward the 12-VP end-game threshold.

| Animal | Instinct Objective |
| --- | --- |
| Eagle | Collect at least **3 Pathfinder tokens**. |
| Bear | Build structures on both a **Forest** tile and a **Highlands** tile. |
| Crocodile | Receive a total of at least **3 Food** from opponents through Crocodile abilities. |
| Wolf | Trigger the maximum **+3 Food** bonus from Pack Synergy. |
| Frog | Immobilise at least **3 opposing animal tokens** during the same round with Sticky Situation. |
| Hare | End your turn with Hares occupying at least **4 different terrain types**. |
| Rat | Begin your turn with an infested tile, then successfully use **Nibble** during that turn. |

## Resources

There are two resource types in WILDLANDS.

### Food

**Food** is the universal currency of survival. It is used to reproduce, pay movement costs, activate certain effects, and support negotiations.

Food can be foraged from any occupied tile. On an animal's primary terrain, collect **1 Food for each of your animal tokens** on that tile.

### Materials

**Materials** are used primarily to construct structures. They can be foraged only from an animal's secondary terrain. On secondary terrain, collect **1 Material for each of your animal tokens** on that tile.

## Turn Structure and Actions

At the start of your turn, first collect structure income by choosing **1 Food or 1 Material per structure**. Then draw cards until you have 5 cards in hand and take exactly **3 actions**.

Each action type can normally be taken only once per turn. **Play a Card** is the only action that may be repeated. For example, a player may take **Move, Play a Card, Play a Card** or **Play a Card, Forage, Play a Card**.

Reactive cards that explicitly say they are played during another player's turn do not consume one of the active player's actions.

### 1. Forage

Collect resources from **each tile** containing at least one of your animal tokens.

A normal Forage action produces a maximum of **6 total resources** before animal-ability bonuses and card effects. If the base yields would exceed 6, choose which 6 resources to collect. Ability bonuses and card effects may raise the final total above this cap.

- **Neither primary nor secondary terrain:** Collect **1 Food per tile**, regardless of how many of your tokens occupy it.
- **Primary terrain:** Collect **1 Food per animal token** you have on that tile.
- **Secondary terrain:** Collect **1 Material per animal token** you have on that tile.
- **Great Watering Hole or Ancient Grove:** Collect **2 resources per tile** in any combination, regardless of how many of your tokens occupy it.

Examples:

- A Crocodile on a Wetlands tile collects **1 Material**, because Wetlands is the Crocodile's secondary terrain.
- A Bear on a Forest tile collects **1 Food**, because Forest is the Bear's primary terrain.
- Two Bears on a River tile collect only **1 Food**, because River is neither the Bear's primary nor secondary terrain.
- Two Bears on a Forest tile collect **2 Food**. A structure on that tile provides separate automatic income at the start of the owner's turn; it does not increase the forage yield.

### 2. Move

Move any number of your tokens up to **1 tile each**, unless an ability or card states otherwise. Spend **1 Food for each token moved**.

- Resolve each token's movement separately. Tokens do not need to move in the same direction or travel the same distance.
- You do not need to move every token.
- You may enter tiles occupied by other players unless an ability prevents it.
- Tokens cannot move through face-down tiles. They must stop on the furthest revealed tile and use an **Explore** action to proceed.
- A tile can contain no more than **6 animal tokens in total**. If a tile is full, no token may enter or move through it.

### 3. Reproduce

Spend Food to place tokens from your reserve onto one or more tiles you currently occupy. You may place up to your animal's **spawn rate**, subject to the number of tokens in your reserve and the six-token tile limit.

Pay the total Food cost shown below, based on the number of your animal sprites already on the board immediately before reproducing. The listed cost pays for the entire Reproduce action, not for each sprite placed.

| Number of Animal Sprites on the Board | Cost for Reproduction |
| ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 1 Food |
| 2 | 1 Food |
| 3 | 1 Food |
| 4 | 2 Food |
| 5 | 3 Food |
| 6 | 5 Food |
| 7 or more | 6 Food |

- You must place each new token on a tile you already occupy.
- You cannot place more tokens than you have in your reserve.
- You cannot place a token on a tile that has reached its occupancy limit.
- Rat structures are placed immediately if reproduction brings the Rat count on a tile to 5 or more.

### 4. Play a Card

Play one card from your hand. Resolve a non-Secret card immediately; place a Secret Card face-down in front of you. Each card played requires one **Play a Card** action unless it is explicitly described as a reactive card or as counting as another action type.

- After resolving a non-Secret card, place it face-up in your discard pile. Secret Cards follow the separate rules in [Secret Card Rules](#secret-card-rules).
- Only the matching animal may activate a **Species Ability Card**.
- A player who cannot activate a Species Ability Card may discard or trade it as described in [Species Ability Cards](#species-ability-cards).
- Unlike other action types, **Play a Card** may be selected more than once during the same turn.

### 5. Build

Construct a structure on a tile you currently occupy if all the following requirements are met:

- The tile is one of your animal's home terrains.
- The tile contains at least as many of your animal tokens as your pack size.
- The tile is not a neutral tile.
- You pay **1 Food and 2 Materials**.
- You have fewer than **3 structures** on the board.

Rats cannot use the **Build** action; see **Infestation** instead.

There is a limit of **3 structures per player** and **1 structure per player per tile**. Different players may each have a structure on the same tile if they independently meet the requirements.

The first time you construct a standard structure on a particular tile, gain **2 VP** immediately and place one of your coloured **Settlement markers** on that tile. A Rat gains **1 VP** instead. The Settlement marker remains on the tile for the rest of the game, even if the structure is destroyed, and shows that you have already scored there.

Rebuilding a structure on a tile containing your Settlement marker awards no VP. Settlement markers are tracked separately for each player: another player may still gain VP for their first structure on that same tile and places a Settlement marker of their own colour. Settlement markers do not count as structures, do not affect tile occupancy, and cannot be removed by card effects.

### 6. Explore

Move one token onto an adjacent face-down tile and flip that tile face-up.

- The exploring token must be adjacent to the face-down tile before the action.
- Immediately collect **1 Pathfinder token**.
- If the revealed tile is one of your home terrains, you may immediately forage on that tile as a bonus without taking a **Forage** action.

## Structures

Structures provide long-term automatic income.

| Attribute | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Build requirement | Home-terrain tile with at least the animal's pack size in tokens |
| Build cost | 1 Food and 2 Materials |
| Passive income | Choose 1 Food or 1 Material per structure at the start of the owner's turn |
| VP when constructed | First structure scored by that player on the tile: 2 VP; Rat structures award 1 VP |
| Limit | 3 structures per player; 1 structure per player per tile |
| Destruction | Only through specified cards or abilities; return the structure to its owner's supply |

Structures do **not** increase the resources gained from a **Forage** action.

### Settlement Markers

Each player has coloured Settlement markers matching their Animal Player Board. When you gain VP for placing your first structure on a tile, put one Settlement marker flat on that tile. If the structure is destroyed, leave the marker behind. If several players have scored structures on the same tile, each keeps a separate marker there.

A practical component layout is:

1. Place the flat Settlement marker on the terrain tile.
2. Place the structure piece on or immediately beside that marker.
3. If the structure is fortified, place a separate shield-shaped Fortification token on top of or directly beside the structure.

### Fortifying Structures

Once during your turn, without spending an action, you may spend **1 Material** to fortify one of your structures. Place a separate **Fortification token** on top of or directly beside the structure so it is clearly associated with that structure. The next effect that would destroy it removes the Fortification token instead, and the structure remains. A structure can have no more than one Fortification token. Fortification tokens return to the general supply when removed; they are different from permanent Settlement markers.

## Card Deck

All players draw from the same central deck. Cards are the primary source of surprise, interaction, and disruption.

### Deck Construction

Use the following deck construction for the balanced short-game format:

- Include Species Ability Cards only for the animals being used in the current game.
- Add **2 copies of each active ability card** for every participating animal. Because each animal has two active abilities, this adds **4 Species Ability Cards per player**.
- Add **6 Generic Cards per player**.
- A **Normal** Generic Card may appear up to twice. A **Powerful** Generic Card may appear only once.
- Aim for approximately one-third of the Generic Cards to be Secret Cards:
  - 3 players: 18 Generic Cards, including approximately 6 Secrets and no more than 4 Powerful cards.
  - 4 players: 24 Generic Cards, including approximately 8 Secrets and no more than 5 Powerful cards.
  - 5 players: 30 Generic Cards, including approximately 10 Secrets and no more than 6 Powerful cards.
  - 6 players: 36 Generic Cards, including approximately 12 Secrets and no more than 7 Powerful cards.
- Choose **1 or 2 Events** for each game and pair each Event with one randomly selected Condition Card. Events and Condition Cards are not shuffled into the central deck.

This creates a **30-, 40-, 50-, or 60-card deck** for 3, 4, 5, or 6 players respectively. When selecting the Generic Cards, include no more than one copy of any effect labelled Powerful.

### Card Categories

#### Generic Cards

Generic Cards may be used by any animal. A non-Secret Generic Card resolves immediately unless its text specifies a duration.

##### Secret Card Rules

- Playing a Secret Card uses one **Play a Card** action. Place it face-down in front of you instead of resolving it immediately.
- A face-down Secret Card is no longer part of your hand and cannot be traded.
- You may have multiple Secret Cards in play at the same time. Their names and effects remain hidden.
- When a Secret Card's printed trigger occurs, you may reveal it immediately without spending another action. Resolve its effect, then place it face-up in your discard pile.
- If you choose not to reveal it when its trigger occurs, it remains face-down unless its text says it expires. A missed trigger cannot be claimed retroactively.
- If a Secret Card reaches its printed expiry without being revealed, place it face-down in your discard pile. It is unresolved.
- When the game ends, do **not** automatically reveal Secret Cards that are still face-down. They are treated as unresolved and do not count toward Chronicler.
- A Secret Card counts as resolved for Chronicler only if it was revealed at a valid trigger and its instructions were carried out as far as possible. Simply placing it face-down, revealing it after the game, or discarding it when it expires does not count.

#### Species Ability Cards

Only the matching animal can activate a Species Ability Card. These cards are required to use the animal's active abilities, although any player may hold them.

If you hold a Species Ability Card that you cannot activate, you have two options:

1. **Discard it:** Use a **Play a Card** action to discard it **face-down** in your discard pile and gain **1 Bone token**. Bone tokens are converted to VP only during final scoring and do not count toward the 12-VP end-game threshold.
2. **Trade it:** Trade it to the matching animal player for resources, other cards, or promises. VP and tokens cannot be traded. Whenever you successfully broker such a trade, gain **1 Diplomatic token**.

During final scoring, every **2 Bone tokens** are worth **1 VP**. An unpaired Bone token is worth 0 VP. A Species Ability Card discarded for a Bone token does not count toward the Chronicler title.

## Events and Conditions

Events affect every player and cannot be avoided. Because their effects are substantial, only 1 or 2 Events are randomly selected for each game.

During setup, pair each selected Event with one random Condition Card. Place each Event face-down beside the board and place its Condition Card face-up on top of it. A Condition Card describes how the Event beneath it is triggered.

### Condition Card Examples

- The first time any player reaches 6 VP, immediately reveal and resolve the Event beneath this card.
- The first time any player places all their animal tokens on the board, immediately reveal and resolve the Event beneath this card.
- When all terrain tiles are face-up, immediately reveal and resolve the Event beneath this card.

### Event Card Examples

- **Plague:** In reverse turn order, beginning with the current player, each player removes half of their animal tokens from the board, rounded down, and returns them to their reserve. For example, a player with 5 tokens on the board removes 2.
- **Doomsday:** Finish the current turn. Beginning with the next player, every other player takes one final turn in normal turn order. After they have finished, the player whose turn triggered Doomsday takes one additional final turn. The game then ends and proceeds to final scoring.

## Trading and Negotiation

Trading and negotiation are free and do not consume an action. They may occur at any point during the active player's turn.

### What Can Be Traded

- Resources: Food and Materials
- Cards from your hand, including Species Ability Cards
- Promises about future actions

Promises are not binding. Players may break them without a mechanical penalty.

VP, Bone tokens, Diplomatic tokens, Pathfinder tokens, and other tokens cannot be traded.

## Scoring and Titles

### Ongoing VP

| Source | VP Earned |
| --- | ---: |
| First standard structure you place on a particular tile | +2 VP immediately |
| First Rat structure you place on a particular tile | +1 VP immediately |
| Completing an Instinct Objective | +3 VP immediately; once per player |
| Every 2 Bone tokens | +1 VP during final scoring |

### Titles

Titles are resolved during final scoring.

| Title | Awarded To | Bonus VP |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Pathfinder | Player with the most Pathfinder tokens | +3 VP |
| Glutton | Player holding the most Food | +3 VP |
| Hoarder | Player holding the most Materials | +3 VP |
| Architect | Player with the most structures still standing | +3 VP |
| Chronicler | Player with the most cards that successfully resolved for their printed effect | +2 VP |
| Broker | Player with the most Diplomatic tokens | +2 VP |

If players tie for a title, each tied player receives half of its bonus VP, rounded down.

For **Chronicler**, count only cards that were played and successfully resolved for their printed effect. Resolved cards are normally kept face-up in their owner's discard pile. Do not count:

- Species Ability Cards discarded face-down for Bone tokens.
- Secret Cards that expired or remained face-down when the game ended.
- A Secret Card revealed only after the game ended.
- Cards that were cancelled before resolving, even if the card's identity had already been revealed.

Do not reveal unresolved Secret Cards merely to determine Chronicler. They remain face-down and are ignored.

## End of the Game

The end of the game is triggered by whichever of the following occurs first:

1. **The round limit is completed:** 5 rounds with 3 players, 4 rounds with 4 players, or 3 rounds with 5–6 players. Proceed directly to final scoring after every player completes the final scheduled round.
2. **The central deck is exhausted.** If the deck runs out during a player's turn, that player finishes the turn. Every other player then takes one final turn in normal turn order, and the triggering player receives one additional final turn.
3. **A player has at least 12 VP at the end of a round.** Title bonuses and Bone-token conversion are not included when checking this threshold. Every player then takes one additional final turn, beginning with the next player in normal turn order; the triggering player takes the last of these turns.

If more than one condition is reached at the same time, use the condition listed first above. After all required turns are completed, convert Bone tokens, award title bonuses, and determine the winner. Do not trigger another final-turn sequence during final turns.

If players tie for the highest total VP, the tied player with the most titles wins. If they are still tied, they share the victory.

## Turn Summary

1. Collect structure income: choose **1 Food or 1 Material per structure**.
2. Draw until you have 5 cards in hand.
3. Take 3 actions. Action types must be different, except that **Play a Card** may be selected more than once.
4. Trade freely at any point during your turn.
